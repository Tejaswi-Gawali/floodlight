Football data

Live football scores are already real — fetched directly from TheSportsDB's free public API, no signup needed. Refreshes automatically every 45 seconds while you're on the Football tab.

Cricket data

Browsers can't read Google's scorecards or scrape scoreboard sites directly (CORS blocks it), but cricket data APIs work fine. Sign up free at cricketdata.org/signup.aspx (formerly CricAPI, no card needed) to get a key.

Free tier is capped at 100 requests/day, so once connected this polls every 5 minutes — sized to last a full 6-7 hour session with some buffer left for reloads. Faster refresh needs a paid plan (see below).

Add this to the end of the artifact URL to open a transparent, chrome-free view sized for an OBS/Streamlabs Browser Source — only the animated scoreboard shows, everything else is hidden:
